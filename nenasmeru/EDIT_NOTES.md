# Edit: Teres & Semi-D 1 Tingkat, Jalan Nenas, Meru Klang

Marketing video, 1080×1920, 30 fps, **50.9 s**, made from photos (no walkthrough clip): the developer's render, elevation and
floor plan, and 6 real site photos. Voiceover is the owner's script read by the cloned voice **"Mad Bi"**.

Status: **PREVIEW** (draft render `renders/nenasmeru-preview-v2.mp4`, low quality, for approval). The final delivery render and the
-14 LUFS master happen after the owner approves. Renders are not in git.

## Concept: "Dari pelan ke rumah" (blueprint → real house)

The whole video lives on an architect's blueprint (blueprint blue `#0a2e84`, a 36/180 px drafting grid, white line work,
JetBrains Mono annotations, Archivo Black headlines, corner crop marks and the site's GPS coordinates as a frame). It opens with the
house being drafted line by line under "RM1,000 JE?", then the real render "develops" under the drawing. The developer's own
drawings are used, inverted to white-on-blue: the front elevation (hook, outro) and the floor plan (room count). Blue stays the
theme the owner asked for; the blueprint treatment is what makes it different from other agents' reels.

- Five transition types, each with its own sound: flash (YES), punch-in cut, blueprint scanner line, whip pan, camera shutter
  (photo montage) and an iris onto the end card; the floor plan zooms through into the map.
- Music: composed for this video with ElevenLabs Music (not the HeyGen track used in the earlier videos). Prompt: "Instrumental
  background music for an upbeat modern property advertisement on TikTok. Bright plucked synth, clean electric guitar chops, warm
  bass, crisp snappy drums and claps, 120 BPM, confident, optimistic, energetic but not aggressive..." Its ring-out ends with the film.
- Sound effects: 8 made for this video with ElevenLabs (pen drafting, rubber stamp, camera shutter, scanner sweep, riser, pen tick,
  whip, typing) plus the bundled pop/click/ping/sparkle/impact/whoosh. 63 cues, each on something that appears.

## How to run

```bash
npm install                                               # hyperframes@0.9.56 (pinned)
(cd /tmp && npm install maplibre-gl@5.24.0) && MAPLIBRE=/tmp/node_modules/maplibre-gl/dist node scripts/nenasmeru/bake_map.cjs   # map.mp4 (not in git)
python3 -I scripts/nenasmeru/tts.py --takes 3             # voice takes (ElevenLabs credits)
python3 -I scripts/nenasmeru/pick_takes.py                # Whisper + pitch scores, keeps the best take of each line
python3 -I scripts/nenasmeru/process_vo.py hook=take3 pkg=take3   # the takes used here (shorter pauses), tempo 1.06
python3 -I scripts/nenasmeru/build.py                     # index.html, captions.html, CUES blocks
cd nenasmeru && npx hyperframes check && npx hyperframes render --quality delivery -o renders/nenasmeru-v1.mp4
# then -14 LUFS / -1.5 dBTP on the audio only (two-pass ffmpeg loudnorm, video copied) -> renders/Jalan-Nenas-Meru-FINAL.mp4
```

`build.py` overwrites `index.html`, `compositions/captions.html` and the CUES blocks; everything else in the scene files is hand-written.
