# Storylines: Rumah Berkembar 1-Tingkat, Jalan Pipit, Telok Panglima Garang

The brief: a vertical video under 60 s, using the developer's copy (price, package, specs,
nearby places), 2 renders, 1 floor plan, 2 site clips and the map pin. No voiceover in the source.

## A. "Rumah dulu, baru tawaran" (BUILT, recommended)

A buyer sees what they're buying first, then why it's worth it, then where it is, then how to book.

- **Device / form:** the house as the hook, then a chain of shots where each shot opens the next
  (the facade pan whips into the side view, the beige wall becomes the package screen, the
  floor plan, the site, the satellite pull-back from the plot out to the Klang Valley).
- **First 2 s:** the front render already on screen with "RUMAH BERKEMBAR 1-TINGKAT" over the sky,
  and the "DARI RM485,700" sticker landing at 1 s.
- **Beats:** house + price → 8 units only → package (booking RM1,000, 3× FREE) → floor plan +
  sizes → site + leasehold + Q4 2027 → map: plot to Shah Alam / Putrajaya / KLCC + highways →
  places nearby (5–19 min) → end card price + phone.
- **Look:** the render's own materials: charcoal facade panels, white concrete, warm beige panel,
  one yellow accent (as in the Meru video).
- **Sound:** the warm acoustic bed from the Meru video, entered at its strongest section so its
  own ending lands on the end card; whooshes on cuts, pops on numbers, one riser into the price.

## B. "RM1,000"

A number lands: "RM1,000" fills the frame in the first second, "itu je untuk booking", then each
FREE item stamps on, then the house is revealed as what that RM1,000 gets you. Type carries the
argument and cuts on the beat. Stronger hook for bargain hunters, weaker on showing the house.

## C. "Dari langit" (bold)

One continuous take: a satellite dive from the Klang Valley down to the plot on Jalan Pipit, the
camera lands on the render, then flies into the floor plan and out to the end card. Most striking,
but the location only means something once you want the house, so the hook is weaker.
